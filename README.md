## The automations behind it

### Booking sync to a shared calendar

[![Booking sync to a shared calendar](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/booking-sync-poster.webp)](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/booking-sync-1920x1080.mp4)

Airbnb and Vrbo booking emails come into one inbox and land on a shared calendar. Changes and cancellations update the calendar, and cleaners get a text.

[Watch the video](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/booking-sync-1920x1080.mp4) · [How it works](https://github.com/RachaelQuisel/la-maison-github/tree/main/workflows/booking-sync)

### Schedule your cleaners by text

[![Schedule your cleaners by text](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/cleaner-job-texts-poster.webp)](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/cleaner-job-texts-1920x1080.mp4)

Your cleaner gets a text, picks the dates they want to clean and is signed up on your shared calendar.

[Watch the video](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/cleaner-job-texts-1920x1080.mp4) · [How it works](https://github.com/RachaelQuisel/la-maison-github/tree/main/workflows/cleaner-signups)

### When your cleaner can't make it, the next one is asked automatically

[![When your cleaner can't make it, the next one is asked automatically](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/cleaning-calendar-poster.webp)](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/cleaning-calendar-1920x1080.mp4)

Each booking creates a cleaning task and a calendar invite. If a cleaner declines, it goes to the next one, and you get a text if a day still needs coverage.

[Watch the video](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/cleaning-calendar-1920x1080.mp4) · [How it works](https://github.com/RachaelQuisel/la-maison-github/tree/main/workflows/cleaning-coverage)

### Door codes for every guest, assigned and revoked automatically

[![Door codes for every guest, assigned and revoked automatically](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/guest-door-codes-poster.webp)](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/guest-door-codes-1920x1080.mp4)

A guest books and gets their own door code, sent in the welcome message, a week before and a day before their stay. Every guest and contractor has a unique code, and each code is revoked after checkout and never reused.

[Watch the video](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/guest-door-codes-1920x1080.mp4) · [How it works](https://github.com/RachaelQuisel/la-maison-github/tree/main/workflows/guest-door-codes)

### When an inspection finds a problem, the right contractor is scheduled automatically

[![When an inspection finds a problem, the right contractor is scheduled automatically](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/property-inspection-poster.webp)](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/property-inspection-1920x1080.mp4)

Your inspector works through a checklist app. When they flag a problem, one tap texts the right contractor, who picks the next open time. Everyone gets a confirmation by text and email, and your maintenance channel is updated.

[Watch the video](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/property-inspection-1920x1080.mp4) · [How it works](https://github.com/RachaelQuisel/la-maison-github/tree/main/workflows/inspection-follow-up)

### Monthly TOT and TBID tax prep

[![Monthly TOT and TBID tax prep](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/tax-prep-poster.webp)](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/tax-prep-1920x1080.mp4)

Payouts and charges from your booking platforms land in a database, a script calculates TOT and TBID, maps the numbers onto the city tax form, and an audit script checks the totals add up.

[Watch the video](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/tax-prep-1920x1080.mp4) · [How it works](https://github.com/RachaelQuisel/la-maison-github/tree/main/workflows/monthly-reporting)

### A morning briefing in Slack so your whole team starts the day on the same page

[![A morning briefing in Slack so your whole team starts the day on the same page](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/ops-today-poster.webp)](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/ops-today-1920x1080.mp4)

Every morning, a briefing is built automatically and posted to your team's Slack channel before the workday starts: alerts, bookings, reviews, money, guest messages and cleaning coverage in one post, so everyone sees the same plan.

[Watch the video](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/ops-today-1920x1080.mp4) · [How it works](https://github.com/RachaelQuisel/la-maison-github/tree/main/workflows/ops-today)

### Every night priced right on Airbnb and VRBO

[![Every night priced right on Airbnb and VRBO](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/daily-pricing-sync-poster.webp)](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/daily-pricing-sync-1920x1080.mp4)

Every night on Airbnb and VRBO stays priced the way you set it. Each morning it checks the next 12 months, fixes any night that's off and posts a short report to your Slack.

[Watch the video](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/daily-pricing-sync-1920x1080.mp4)

## Also built: a task tool for teams

### Friendly task tracker

[![Friendly task tracker](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/friendly-task-tracker-poster.webp)](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/friendly-task-tracker.mp4)

A task tracker for property managers, cleaners or inspectors working across many houses. Each house shows whether its tasks are on track, due soon or overdue, and finished tasks earn points. My own rental is a single home, but this tool is built for teams covering several properties.

[Watch the video](https://airbnbai.rent/assets/friendly-task-tracker.mp4) · [Source on GitHub](https://github.com/RachaelQuisel/Airtable-AI-Webinar)

## Plugins I built for Claude, Codex and other LLMs

- **[GitHub Portfolio Builder](https://github.com/RachaelQuisel/github-portfolio-builder)** reviews a profile or repository as portfolio evidence, checks links and claims and ranks a few
  specific improvements. Its source and Claude Code install path are public.
- **[Questions Worth Asking](https://github.com/RachaelQuisel/questions-worth-asking)** finds unanswered questions, conflicting instructions and unclear ownership in meeting
  transcripts. Each suggested question points back to the passage that prompted it.
- **[Turn Off AI Data Sharing](https://github.com/RachaelQuisel/turn-off-ai-data-sharing)** compares documented AI product privacy defaults with settings a user can verify in their own
  accounts. It separates documented defaults, observed settings and unresolved gaps.
